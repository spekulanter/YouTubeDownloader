|---|---|---|
| Tauri | https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri | Apache-2.0 OR MIT |
| React | https://github.com/facebook/react | MIT |
| yt-dlp | https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp | Unlicense, plus third-party notices in official executable distributions |
| yt-dlp-ejs | https://github.com/yt-dlp/ejs | Unlicense with bundled third-party ISC/MIT components |

The build script verifies archive integrity but does not replace a release compliance review. Add the license texts from the exact staged release payloads to a public distribution and provide source/source-offer information where required.
